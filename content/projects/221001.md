# Data Privacy Deep Dive
An argumentative exploration of data privacy, and what companies know about you.

## Details
This project explores the lesser known facts about data collection, with the goal of educating the viewer on what companies truly know about them. It is designed in a way to make the viewer question and understand the implications of data privacy.
